Zum Schluß

In Closing · Robert Schumann, Myrthen, Op. 25, No. 26 (1840)
Poem: Friedrich Rückert (1788–1866), from Liebesfrühling

Hier in diesen erdbeklomm'nen
Lüften, wo die Wehmut taut,
hab' ich dir den unvollkomm'nen
Kranz geflochten, Schwester, Braut!
Here in this earth-weighed
air, where sadness falls like dew,
I have woven for you the imperfect
wreath, sister, bride!
Wenn uns, droben aufgenommen,
Gottes Sonn' entgegenschaut,
wird die Liebe den vollkomm'nen
Kranz uns flechten, Schwester, Braut!
When, taken up above,
we are met by God's sun,
love will weave for us
the perfect wreath, sister, bride!
About the song. The last song of Myrthen. On earth the poet can only weave an imperfect wreath for his 'sister, bride'; in heaven love will weave the perfect one. 'Erdbeklommen' means oppressed, made anxious by the earth; 'wo die Wehmut taut' is where sadness falls like dew. Rückert wrote 'Wenn uns droben aufgenommnen' (when on us, taken up above, God's sun looks); Schumann sets 'aufgenommen'.
