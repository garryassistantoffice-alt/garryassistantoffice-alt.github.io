Der stürmische Morgen

The Stormy Morning · Franz Schubert, Winterreise, D. 911, No. 18 (1827)
Poem: Wilhelm Müller (1794–1827)

Wie hat der Sturm zerrissen
Des Himmels graues Kleid!
Die Wolkenfetzen flattern
Umher in mattem Streit.
How the storm has torn
the grey garment of the sky!
The tatters of cloud flutter
about in weary strife.
Und rote Feuerflammen
Zieh'n zwischen ihnen hin;
Das nenn' ich einen Morgen
So recht nach meinem Sinn!
And red flames of fire
streak between them;
that is what I call a morning
just to my liking!
Mein Herz sieht an dem Himmel
Gemalt sein eig'nes Bild –
Es ist nichts als der Winter,
Der Winter kalt und wild!
My heart sees, painted on the sky,
its own image:
it is nothing but winter,
winter, cold and wild!
About the song. A storm has torn the grey clouds to shreds and red flames of dawn shoot between them: a morning after the traveller's own heart, which sees itself painted in the sky. 'Wolkenfetzen' are tatters of cloud; 'in mattem Streit' is a weary, half-hearted battle. The text is Müller's word for word.
