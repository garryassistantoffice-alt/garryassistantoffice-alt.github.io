Du bist wie eine Blume

You Are Like a Flower · Robert Schumann, Myrthen, Op. 25, No. 24 (1840)
Poem: Heinrich Heine (1797–1856), from Die Heimkehr

Du bist wie eine Blume
So hold und schön und rein;
Ich schau' dich an, und Wehmut
Schleicht mir ins Herz hinein.
You are like a flower,
so sweet and lovely and pure;
I look at you, and sadness
steals into my heart.
Mir ist, als ob ich die Hände
Aufs Haupt dir legen sollt',
Betend, daß Gott dich erhalte
So rein und schön und hold.
I feel as if my hands
I ought to lay upon your head,
praying that God may keep you
so pure and lovely and sweet.
About the song. From Myrthen, the song collection Schumann gave Clara Wieck as a wedding present in 1840. Heine's eight lines turn admiration into a kind of blessing: the lover wants to lay his hands on the girl's head and pray that she stays as she is. 'Hold' means sweet, gracious; 'Wehmut' is a wistful sadness.
