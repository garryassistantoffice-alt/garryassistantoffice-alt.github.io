Come raggio di sol

As a Ray of Sunlight · Antonio Caldara, Aria (early 18th century; source uncertain)
Poem: Anonymous

Come raggio di sol mite e sereno
sovra placidi flutti si riposa,
mentre del mare nel profondo seno
sta la tempesta ascosa:
As a ray of sunlight, gentle and serene,
rests upon calm waves,
while in the deep bosom of the sea
the storm lies hidden:
così riso talor gaio e pacato
di contento, di gioia un labbro infiora,
mentre nel suo segreto il cor piagato
s'angoscia e si martora.
so at times a laugh, cheerful and calm,
makes lips bloom with contentment, with joy,
while in its secret the wounded heart
is anguished and tormented.
About the song. A single extended simile: sunlight rests on calm water while a storm hides in the depths, and in the same way a smiling face can hide a wounded heart. The original source of the aria has not been identified with certainty. 'Labbro' (lip) stands for the mouth or face, and 'si martora' is an old form meaning 'torments itself'.
