Feldeinsamkeit

Solitude in the Fields · Johannes Brahms, Op. 86, No. 2 (1879)
Poem: Hermann Allmers (1821–1902)

Ich ruhe still im hohen grünen Gras
Und sende lange meinen Blick nach oben,
Von Grillen rings umschwirrt ohn' Unterlaß,
Von Himmelsbläue wundersam umwoben.
I rest quietly in the tall green grass
and send my gaze upward for a long time,
with crickets chirring all around me without pause,
wondrously woven round by the blue of the sky.
Die schönen weißen Wolken ziehn dahin
Durchs tiefe Blau, wie schöne stille Träume;
Mir ist, als ob ich längst gestorben bin
Und ziehe selig mit durch ew'ge Räume.
The lovely white clouds drift by
through the deep blue, like lovely quiet dreams;
I feel as if I had died long ago
and were drifting blissfully with them through eternal space.
About the song. A summer day in the grass, the sky and the drifting clouds, until the poet feels he has died and is floating with them. Brahms set the poem as printed in Allmers's Dichtungen of 1860; a later edition (1892) begins the second stanza 'Und schöne, weiße Wolken'. 'Grillen' are crickets.
