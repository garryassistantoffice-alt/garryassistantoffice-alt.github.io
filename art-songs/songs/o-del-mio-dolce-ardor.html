O del mio dolce ardor

O Longed-for Object of My Sweet Passion · Christoph Willibald Gluck, Paride ed Elena (Vienna, 1770)
Poem: Ranieri de’ Calzabigi (1714–1795)

O del mio dolce ardor
bramato oggetto,
l'aura che tu respiri,
alfin respiro.
O, of my sweet passion
the longed-for object,
the air that you breathe
at last I breathe.
Ovunque il guardo io giro,
le tue vaghe sembianze
amore in me dipinge:
il mio pensier si finge
le più liete speranze;
e nel desio che così m'empie il petto
cerco te, chiamo te, spero e sospiro.
Wherever I turn my gaze,
your lovely features
love paints within me:
my thoughts imagine
the happiest hopes;
and in the longing that so fills my breast
I seek you, I call you, I hope and I sigh.
About the song. Paris's opening aria in Gluck's Paride ed Elena: he has landed near Sparta and already breathes the air that Helen breathes. 'Vaghe' means lovely, not vague, and 'si finge' means 'pictures to itself', 'imagines' (not 'pretends').
