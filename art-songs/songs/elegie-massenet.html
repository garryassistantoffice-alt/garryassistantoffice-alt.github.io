Élégie

Elegy · Jules Massenet, Mélodie
Poem: Louis Gallet (1835–1898)

Ô doux printemps d'autrefois, vertes saisons,
Vous avez fui pour toujours!
Je ne vois plus le ciel bleu,
Je n'entends plus les chants joyeux des oiseaux!
O sweet springtime of former days, green seasons,
you have fled forever!
I no longer see the blue sky,
I no longer hear the joyful songs of the birds!
En emportant mon bonheur,
Ô bien-aimé, tu t'en es allé!
Et c'est en vain que revient le printemps!
Oui, sans retour, avec toi, le gai soleil,
Les jours riants sont partis!
Comme en mon cœur tout est sombre et glacé!
Tout est flétri pour toujours!
Taking my happiness with you,
O beloved, you have gone away!
And it is in vain that spring returns!
Yes, never to return, with you the cheerful sun
and the smiling days have gone!
How dark and frozen all is in my heart!
All is withered forever!
About the song. A lament for a lost love: spring returns, but the beloved, and with him the sun and the smiling days, will not. The melody also exists as a piano piece (Mélodie, Op. 10 No. 5) and was used in Massenet's incidental music to Les Érinnyes; it is widely known in instrumental versions.
