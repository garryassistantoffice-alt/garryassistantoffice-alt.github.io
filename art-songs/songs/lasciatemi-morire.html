Lasciatemi morire!

Let Me Die! · Claudio Monteverdi, L'Arianna (Mantua, 1608), Lamento d'Arianna
Poem: Ottavio Rinuccini (1562/63–1621)

Lasciatemi morire!
E che volete voi che mi conforte
in così dura sorte,
in così gran martire?
Lasciatemi morire!
Let me die!
And what do you think could comfort me
in so harsh a fate,
in such great suffering?
Let me die!
About the song. The opening of Ariadne's lament, abandoned by Theseus on the island of Naxos; the lament is the only music to survive from Monteverdi's opera L'Arianna and was famous in its own day. 'Conforte' is an old form of 'conforti': literally 'what do you want that might comfort me?', meaning 'what could possibly comfort me?'.
