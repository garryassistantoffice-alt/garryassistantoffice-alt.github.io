Was will die einsame Träne

What Does the Lonely Tear Want · Robert Schumann, Myrthen, Op. 25, No. 21 (1840)
Poem: Heinrich Heine (1797–1856), from Die Heimkehr

Was will die einsame Träne?
Sie trübt mir ja den Blick.
Sie blieb aus alten Zeiten
in meinem Auge zurück.
What does the lonely tear want?
It only blurs my sight.
It stayed behind from old times
in my eye.
Sie hatte viel leuchtende Schwestern,
die alle zerflossen sind,
mit meinen Qualen und Freuden,
zerflossen in Nacht und Wind.
It had many shining sisters,
who have all melted away,
with my torments and joys,
melted away into night and wind.
Wie Nebel sind auch zerflossen
die blauen Sternelein,
die mir jene Freuden und Qualen
gelächelt ins Herz hinein.
Like mist they too have melted away,
the little blue stars
that smiled those joys and torments
into my heart.
Ach, meine Liebe selber
zerfloß wie eitel Hauch!
Du alte, einsame Träne,
zerfließe jetzunder auch!
Ah, my love itself
melted away like a mere breath!
You old, lonely tear,
melt away now too!
About the song. One tear is left over from an old love; all its 'sisters' have long since flowed away, and the poet asks it to go too. The 'little blue stars' are the beloved's eyes. 'Eitel' here means mere, empty; 'jetzunder' is an old form of 'jetzt', now.
