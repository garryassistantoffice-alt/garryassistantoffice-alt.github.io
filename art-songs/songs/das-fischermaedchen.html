Das Fischermädchen

The Fisher-Girl · Franz Schubert, Schwanengesang, D. 957, No. 10 (1828)
Poem: Heinrich Heine (1797–1856), from Die Heimkehr

Du schönes Fischermädchen,
Treibe den Kahn ans Land;
Komm zu mir und setze dich nieder,
Wir kosen Hand in Hand.
You pretty fisher-girl,
bring your boat to land;
come to me and sit down,
we'll whisper sweet things, hand in hand.
Leg' an mein Herz dein Köpfchen
Und fürchte dich nicht zu sehr;
Vertraust du dich doch sorglos
Täglich dem wilden Meer.
Lay your little head on my heart
and don't be too afraid;
after all, you trust yourself without a care
every day to the wild sea.
Mein Herz gleicht ganz dem Meere,
Hat Sturm und Ebb' und Flut,
Und manche schöne Perle
In seiner Tiefe ruht.
My heart is just like the sea,
it has storm and ebb and flood,
and many a lovely pearl
rests in its depths.
About the song. A light barcarolle: the poet calls a fisher-girl to bring her boat ashore and sit with him, telling her not to fear him, since she trusts herself every day to the wild sea, and his heart is just like the sea. 'Kosen' means to caress or whisper endearments.
