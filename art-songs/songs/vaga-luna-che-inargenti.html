Vaga luna, che inargenti

Lovely Moon, You Who Silver · Vincenzo Bellini, Tre ariette, No. 3
Poem: Anonymous

Vaga luna, che inargenti
queste rive e questi fiori
ed inspiri agli elementi
il linguaggio dell'amor;
testimonio or sei tu sola
del mio fervido desir,
ed a lei che m'innamora
conta i palpiti e i sospir.
Lovely moon, you who silver
these shores and these flowers
and breathe into the elements
the language of love:
you alone are now the witness
of my ardent longing;
and to her who makes me love
tell my heartbeats and my sighs.
Dille pur che lontananza
il mio duol non può lenir,
che se nutro una speranza,
ella è sol nell'avvenir.
Dille pur che giorno e sera
conto l'ore del dolor,
che una speme lusinghiera
mi conforta nell'amor.
Tell her that distance
cannot soothe my grief,
that if I nurture a hope,
it lies only in the future.
Tell her that day and night
I count the hours of sorrow,
that an alluring hope
comforts me in my love.
About the song. The third of Bellini's Tre ariette; the author of the words is unknown. A lover far from his beloved asks the moon, the only witness of his longing, to carry his heartbeats and sighs to her and to tell her that his hope lies in the future. Some editions print 'ispiri' for 'inspiri' (you breathe into); the meaning is the same. 'Speme lusinghiera' is an alluring, flattering hope.
