Der Schmied

The Blacksmith · Johannes Brahms, Op. 19, No. 4 (1862)
Poem: Ludwig Uhland (1787–1862)

Ich hör' meinen Schatz,
den Hammer er schwinget,
das rauschet, das klinget,
das dringt in die Weite
wie Glockengeläute
durch Gassen und Platz.
I hear my sweetheart,
he swings his hammer,
it roars, it rings,
it carries far
like the pealing of bells
through the streets and the square.
Am schwarzen Kamin,
da sitzet mein Lieber,
doch geh' ich vorüber,
die Bälge dann sausen,
die Flammen aufbrausen
und lodern um ihn.
At the black hearth
sits my beloved;
but when I walk past,
then the bellows roar,
the flames blaze up
and flare around him.
About the song. A girl hears her sweetheart the blacksmith at work all over town; when she walks past his forge, the bellows roar and the flames flare up around him. The words are as in Uhland's Gedichte. 'Bälge' are the bellows that blow air into the fire.
