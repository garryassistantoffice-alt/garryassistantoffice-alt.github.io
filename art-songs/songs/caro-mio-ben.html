Caro mio ben

My Dear Beloved · Attributed to Tommaso or Giuseppe Giordani, Arietta (late 18th century)
Poem: Anonymous

Caro mio ben,
credimi almen,
senza di te
languisce il cor.
My dear beloved,
believe me at least:
without you
my heart languishes.
Il tuo fedel
sospira ognor.
Cessa, crudel,
tanto rigor!
Your faithful one
sighs always.
Cease, cruel one,
such harshness!
About the song. One of the most-sung songs of the Italian anthology. Its composer is uncertain: it was long credited to Giuseppe Giordani (c. 1751–1798), but recent scholarship more often gives it to Tommaso Giordani, and some writers to Tommaso's father. 'Caro mio ben' is literally 'my dear good', a standard way of saying 'my beloved'.
