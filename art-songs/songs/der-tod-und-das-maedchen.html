Der Tod und das Mädchen

Death and the Maiden · Franz Schubert, D. 531, Op. 7 No. 3 (1817)
Poem: Matthias Claudius (1740–1815)

Vorüber! ach, vorüber!
Geh, wilder Knochenmann!
Ich bin noch jung, geh, Lieber!
Und rühre mich nicht an.
Pass by! Ah, pass by!
Go, savage man of bones!
I am still young; go, dear one,
and do not touch me.
Gib deine Hand, du schön und zart Gebild!
Bin Freund und komme nicht zu strafen.
Sei gutes Muts! ich bin nicht wild,
Sollst sanft in meinen Armen schlafen!
Give me your hand, you lovely and tender creature!
I am a friend, and do not come to punish.
Be of good cheer! I am not savage;
you shall sleep gently in my arms!
About the song. A dialogue: in the first stanza a girl begs Death to pass her by; in the second Death answers gently that he comes as a friend, not to punish, and that she will sleep softly in his arms. The Peters edition marks the speakers '(Das Mädchen)' and '(Der Tod)'. 'Knochenmann' (bone man) is Death as a skeleton; 'Gebild' is a creature, a form.
