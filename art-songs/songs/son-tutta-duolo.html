Son tutta duolo

I Am All Grief · Alessandro Scarlatti, Aria
Poem: Anonymous

Son tutta duolo,
non ho che affanni,
e mi dà morte
pena crudel.
I am all grief,
I have nothing but sorrows,
and a cruel pain
is giving me death.
E per me solo
sono tiranni
gli astri, la sorte,
i numi, il ciel.
And for me alone
are tyrants
the stars, fate,
the gods, heaven.
About the song. A short lament: the singer has nothing but sorrow, a cruel pain is killing her, and the stars, fate, the gods and heaven are tyrants to her alone. 'Duolo' is a poetic word for grief. Text as printed in Parisotti's anthology (1894).
