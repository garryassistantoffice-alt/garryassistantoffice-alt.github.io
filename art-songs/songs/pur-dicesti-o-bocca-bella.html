Pur dicesti, o bocca bella

At Last You Said It, Lovely Mouth · Antonio Lotti, Arietta
Poem: Anonymous

Pur dicesti, o bocca bella,
quel soave e caro sì,
che fa tutto il mio piacer.
At last you said, O lovely mouth,
that sweet and precious yes
which makes up all my pleasure.
Per onor di sua facella
con un bacio Amor t'aprì,
dolce fonte del goder.
To honour his torch,
Love opened you with a kiss,
sweet fountain of delight.
About the song. The lover's beloved has finally said yes: the 'sweet and dear yes' that is all his pleasure. In the second stanza Love opened her mouth with a kiss in honour of his torch, 'facella' (a little torch, the emblem of Cupid). Text as printed in Parisotti's anthology (1894).
