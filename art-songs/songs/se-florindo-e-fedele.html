Se Florindo è fedele

If Florindo Is Faithful · Alessandro Scarlatti, Arietta
Poem: Anonymous

Se Florindo è fedele
io m'innamorerò.
If Florindo is faithful,
I shall fall in love.
Potrà ben l'arco tendere
il faretrato arcier,
ch'io mi saprò difendere
d'un guardo lusinghier.
The archer with the quiver
may well draw his bow,
for I shall know how to defend myself
from an alluring glance.
Preghi, pianti e querele,
io non ascolterò,
ma se sarà fedele
io m'innamorerò.
Prayers, tears and complaints
I shall not listen to;
but if he is faithful,
I shall fall in love.
About the song. A shepherdess promises to fall in love with Florindo, but only if he proves faithful: until then she will resist Cupid, 'the archer with the quiver', and ignore all prayers and tears. 'Faretrato arcier' is the quiver-bearing archer; 'guardo lusinghier' is a flattering, alluring glance. Text as printed in Parisotti's anthology (1894).
