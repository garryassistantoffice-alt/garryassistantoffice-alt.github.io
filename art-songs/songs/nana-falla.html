Nana

Lullaby · Manuel de Falla, Siete canciones populares españolas, No. 5 (1914)
Poem: Spanish folk text

Duérmete, niño, duerme,
duerme, mi alma,
duérmete, lucerito
de la mañana.
Go to sleep, child, sleep,
sleep, my soul,
go to sleep, little star
of the morning.
Nanita, nana,
nanita, nana.
Lullaby, lulla,
lullaby, lulla.
About the song. A lullaby; 'nana' and 'nanita' are the Spanish words for a cradle song, used here as the refrain. 'Lucerito' is a diminutive of 'lucero', the bright morning star. Falla repeats the last two lines of the first stanza at the end.
