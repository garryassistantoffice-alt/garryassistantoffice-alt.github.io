Der greise Kopf

The Grey Head · Franz Schubert, Winterreise, D. 911, No. 14 (1827)
Poem: Wilhelm Müller (1794–1827)

Der Reif hat einen weißen Schein
Mir über's Haar gestreuet;
Da glaubt' ich schon ein Greis zu sein
Und hab' mich sehr gefreuet.
The frost has scattered a white sheen
over my hair;
I thought I was already an old man
and was very glad.
Doch bald ist er hinweggetaut,
Hab' wieder schwarze Haare,
Daß mir's vor meiner Jugend graut –
Wie weit noch bis zur Bahre!
But soon it thawed away;
I have black hair again,
so that I shudder at my youth.
How far it still is to the bier!
Vom Abendrot zum Morgenlicht
Ward mancher Kopf zum Greise.
Wer glaubt's? Und meiner ward es nicht
Auf dieser ganzen Reise!
Between sunset and morning light
many a head has turned grey.
Who would believe it? And mine did not,
on this whole journey!
About the song. Hoar frost has whitened the traveller's hair, and for a moment he is glad to think himself an old man, near the end; then it thaws, his hair is black again, and he shudders at how long his youth may last. 'Greis' is an old man; 'Bahre' is the bier. Müller has 'Der Reif hatt'' and 'Da meint' ich' where Schubert sets 'hat' and 'Da glaubt' ich'.
