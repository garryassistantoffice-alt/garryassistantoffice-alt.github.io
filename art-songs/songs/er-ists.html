Er ist's

Spring Is Here · Hugo Wolf, Mörike-Lieder, No. 6 (1888)
Poem: Eduard Mörike (1804–1875)

Frühling läßt sein blaues Band
Wieder flattern durch die Lüfte;
Süße, wohlbekannte Düfte
Streifen ahnungsvoll das Land.
Veilchen träumen schon,
Wollen balde kommen.
Horch, von fern ein leiser Harfenton!
Frühling, ja du bist's!
Dich hab' ich vernommen!
Spring lets his blue ribbon
flutter through the air again;
sweet, familiar scents
brush the land, full of promise.
Violets are already dreaming,
they mean to come soon.
Listen, from far off, the soft sound of a harp!
Spring, yes, it is you!
I have heard you!
About the song. The title means literally 'It is he': 'he' is spring (der Frühling, masculine in German), recognised by his blue ribbon of sky, his scents and a distant harp. Wolf's piano rushes and flutters throughout, and he repeats 'Frühling, ja du bist's!', which we print once. 'Balde' is an old form of 'bald', soon.
