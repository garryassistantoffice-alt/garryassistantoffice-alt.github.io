Lasciar d'amarti

To Stop Loving You · Francesco Gasparini, Arietta
Poem: Anonymous

Lasciar d'amarti
per non penar,
caro mio bene,
non si può far.
To stop loving you
so as not to suffer,
my dearest love,
cannot be done.
A forza di pene,
di strali e catene,
non voglio lasciarti,
ti voglio adorar.
Whatever the pains,
the arrows and chains,
I will not leave you,
I want to adore you.
About the song. Parisotti prints this as a companion 'fragment from the second cantata' to Caro laccio: to stop loving in order to stop suffering is impossible, and in spite of all Cupid's pains, arrows and chains the lover will go on adoring. 'Non si può far' is 'it cannot be done'.
