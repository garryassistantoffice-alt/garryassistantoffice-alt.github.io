Auf einer Burg

In a Castle · Robert Schumann, Liederkreis, Op. 39, No. 7 (1840)
Poem: Joseph von Eichendorff (1788–1857)

Eingeschlafen auf der Lauer
oben ist der alte Ritter;
drüber gehen Regenschauer,
und der Wald rauscht durch das Gitter.
Fallen asleep on the watch
up there is the old knight;
rain showers pass over him,
and the forest rustles through the grating.
Eingewachsen Bart und Haare
und versteinert Brust und Krause,
sitzt er viele hundert Jahre
oben in der stillen Klause.
His beard and hair grown into the stone,
his breast and ruff turned to stone,
he sits for many hundreds of years
up in the silent cell.
Draußen ist es still und friedlich,
alle sind ins Tal gezogen,
Waldesvögel einsam singen
in den leeren Fensterbogen.
Outside it is still and peaceful,
everyone has gone down to the valley;
forest birds sing alone
in the empty window arches.
Eine Hochzeit fährt da unten
auf dem Rhein im Sonnenscheine,
Musikanten spielen munter,
und die schöne Braut, die weinet.
A wedding party sails down there
on the Rhine in the sunshine,
musicians play merrily,
and the lovely bride, she weeps.
About the song. An old knight has fallen asleep at his post and turned to stone in an empty castle above the Rhine, while far below a wedding party sails past and the bride weeps. 'Auf der Lauer' means on the lookout; 'Krause' is the ruff of his collar; a 'Klause' is a hermit's cell. Schumann wrote 'drüben' (over there) where the poem has 'drüber' (over it); Friedlaender's Peters edition prints the poet's 'drüber'.
