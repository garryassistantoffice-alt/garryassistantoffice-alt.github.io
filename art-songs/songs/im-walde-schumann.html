Im Walde

In the Forest · Robert Schumann, Liederkreis, Op. 39, No. 11 (1840)
Poem: Joseph von Eichendorff (1788–1857)

Es zog eine Hochzeit den Berg entlang,
ich hörte die Vögel schlagen,
da blitzten viel Reiter, das Waldhorn klang,
das war ein lustiges Jagen!
A wedding procession moved along the mountain,
I heard the birds singing,
then many riders flashed by, the hunting horn sounded,
that was a merry hunt!
Und eh' ich's gedacht, war alles verhallt,
die Nacht bedecket die Runde,
nur von den Bergen noch rauschet der Wald,
und mich schauert's im Herzensgrunde.
And before I knew it, everything had died away,
night covers the land around,
only from the mountains the forest still rustles,
and a shudder goes through the depths of my heart.
About the song. A wedding procession and a hunt pass by in a flash of riders and horn calls; then night falls and the listener is left alone with the forest and a sudden shudder. 'Die Runde' is the land all around; 'blitzten' means the riders flashed and glinted. Eichendorff wrote 'Und mich schauert im Herzensgrunde'; Schumann sets 'mich schauert's'.
