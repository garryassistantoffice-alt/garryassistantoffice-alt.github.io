Pietà, Signore!

Mercy, Lord! · Probably 19th century; attributed to Alessandro Stradella, Aria di chiesa (sacred aria)
Poem: Anonymous

Pietà, Signore,
di me dolente!
Signor, pietà,
se a te giunge il mio pregar;
non mi punisca il tuo rigor,
meno severi, clementi ognora,
volgi i tuoi sguardi sopra di me.
Mercy, Lord,
on me in my sorrow!
Lord, mercy,
if my prayer reaches you;
let your severity not punish me,
less stern, merciful always,
turn your gaze upon me.
Non fia mai che nell'inferno
sia dannato
nel fuoco eterno
dal tuo rigor.
May it never be that in hell
I am damned
to the eternal fire
by your severity.
Gran Dio, giammai
sia dannato
nel fuoco eterno
dal tuo rigor.
Great God, may I never
be damned
to the eternal fire
by your severity.
About the song. Presented as a church aria by Stradella at one of François-Joseph Fétis's historical concerts in Paris in 1833, this piece is almost certainly a 19th-century composition in an older style; Fétis himself, Louis Niedermeyer and Rossini have been suggested as its composer, without proof. A sinner begs for mercy and to be spared eternal fire. 'Fia' is an old future form of 'essere' (= sarà, will be): 'non fia mai' means 'may it never be'.
