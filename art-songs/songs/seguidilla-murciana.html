Seguidilla murciana

Seguidilla from Murcia · Manuel de Falla, Siete canciones populares españolas, No. 2 (1914)
Poem: Spanish folk text

Cualquiera que el tejado
tenga de vidrio,
no debe tirar piedras
al del vecino.
Whoever has a roof
made of glass
should not throw stones
at the neighbour's.
Arrieros semos;
¡puede que en el camino
nos encontremos!
We are muleteers;
we may well meet
on the road!
Por tu mucha inconstancia
yo te comparo
con peseta que corre
de mano en mano;
For your great fickleness
I compare you
to a peseta that passes
from hand to hand;
que al fin se borra,
y creyéndola falsa
¡nadie la toma!
which in the end wears smooth,
and, thinking it false,
nobody will take it!
About the song. A string of proverbs thrown at a fickle lover: people with glass roofs should not throw stones; we are all muleteers and our paths will cross again; and you are like a coin passed from hand to hand until it is rubbed smooth and nobody will take it. 'Semos' is a dialect form of 'somos' (we are). A seguidilla is a Spanish dance-song; this one is from Murcia.
