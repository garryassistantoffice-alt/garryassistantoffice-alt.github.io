Ici-bas!

Here Below · Gabriel Fauré, Op. 8, No. 3
Poem: Sully Prudhomme (1839–1907), from Stances et poèmes

Ici-bas tous les lilas meurent,
Tous les chants des oiseaux sont courts;
Je rêve aux étés qui demeurent
Toujours!
Here below all the lilacs die,
all the songs of the birds are short;
I dream of summers that stay
forever!
Ici-bas les lèvres effleurent
Sans rien laisser de leur velours;
Je rêve aux baisers qui demeurent
Toujours!
Here below lips only brush
and leave nothing of their velvet;
I dream of kisses that stay
forever!
Ici-bas tous les hommes pleurent
Leurs amitiés ou leurs amours;
Je rêve aux couples qui demeurent
Toujours!
Here below all people mourn
their friendships or their loves;
I dream of couples who stay together
forever!
About the song. Three short stanzas set what passes 'here below' (lilacs, kisses, loves) against a dream of what lasts forever. The poem, 'Ici-bas' in Sully Prudhomme's Stances et poèmes (1865), sets the final 'Toujours...' apart as a line of one word; Fauré's title adds an exclamation mark. 'Effleurer': to brush lightly, to touch only the surface.
