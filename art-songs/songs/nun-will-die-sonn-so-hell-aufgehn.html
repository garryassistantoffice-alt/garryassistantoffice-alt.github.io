Nun will die Sonn' so hell aufgeh'n

Now the Sun Will Rise as Brightly · Gustav Mahler, Kindertotenlieder, No. 1 (published 1905)
Poem: Friedrich Rückert (1788–1866)

Nun will die Sonn' so hell aufgeh'n,
als sei kein Unglück die Nacht gescheh'n!
Now the sun will rise as brightly
as if no misfortune had happened in the night!
Das Unglück geschah nur mir allein!
Die Sonne, sie scheinet allgemein!
The misfortune happened to me alone!
The sun, it shines on everyone!
Du mußt nicht die Nacht in dir verschränken,
mußt sie ins ew'ge Licht versenken!
You must not lock the night up inside you,
you must sink it into the eternal light!
Ein Lämplein verlosch in meinem Zelt!
Heil sei dem Freudenlicht der Welt!
A little lamp has gone out in my tent!
Hail to the joyful light of the world!
About the song. The first of Mahler's five Songs on the Death of Children, on poems that Rückert left unpublished; they appeared from his papers in 1872. The sun rises as if nothing had happened: the loss is the speaker's alone, and he tells himself to sink his night into the eternal light. 'Verschränken' means to shut in, lock up. Rückert's text has 'Sonne', 'auch mir allein', 'Du mußt die Nacht nicht', 'Lämpchen' and 'Freudenlichte'; Mahler sets it as here.
