Sérénade

Serenade · Charles Gounod, Mélodie
Poem: Victor Hugo (1802–1885)

Quand tu chantes bercée
Le soir entre mes bras,
Entends-tu ma pensée
Qui te répond tout bas?
Ton doux chant me rappelle
Les plus beaux de mes jours.
Ah! chantez, chantez, ma belle,
Chantez, chantez toujours!
When you sing, cradled
in my arms in the evening,
do you hear my thought
answering you softly?
Your sweet song recalls to me
the most beautiful of my days.
Ah! sing, sing, my lovely one,
sing, sing forever!
Quand tu ris, sur ta bouche
L'amour s'épanouit,
Et soudain le farouche
Soupçon s'évanouit.
Ah! le rire fidèle
Prouve un cœur sans détours.
Ah! riez, riez, ma belle,
Riez, riez toujours!
When you laugh, on your lips
love blossoms,
and suddenly fierce
suspicion vanishes.
Ah! faithful laughter
proves a heart without guile.
Ah! laugh, laugh, my lovely one,
laugh, laugh forever!
Quand tu dors, calme et pure,
Dans l'ombre sous mes yeux,
Ton haleine murmure
Des mots harmonieux.
Ton beau corps se révèle
Sans voile et sans atours.
Ah! dormez, dormez, ma belle,
Dormez, dormez toujours!
When you sleep, calm and pure,
in the shadow beneath my eyes,
your breath murmurs
harmonious words.
Your lovely body is revealed
without veil and without finery.
Ah! sleep, sleep, my lovely one,
sleep, sleep forever!
About the song. A strophic serenade in three verses: singing, laughing, sleeping. The lover addresses her as 'tu' in the verses but switches to the more formal, more ceremonious 'vous' in the refrains ('chantez', 'riez', 'dormez'), a switch English cannot show. Each refrain is sung with more repetitions than printed; 'farouche' is fierce or wild, 'atours' is finery.
