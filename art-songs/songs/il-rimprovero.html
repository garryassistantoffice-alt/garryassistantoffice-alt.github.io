Il rimprovero

The Reproach · Gioachino Rossini, Soirées musicales, No. 2 (publ. 1835)
Poem: Pietro Metastasio (1698–1782)

Mi lagnerò tacendo
della mia sorte amara,
ah ch'io non t'ami, o cara,
non lo sperar da me.
I shall lament in silence
my bitter fate;
ah, that I should not love you, my dear,
do not hope for that from me.
Crudel, perché fin'ora
farmi penar così?
Cruel one, why until now
have you made me suffer so?
About the song. Rossini set these lines of Metastasio, an aria text from the opera libretto Siroe, many times. The lover will suffer in silence but will never stop loving. Metastasio wrote 'Ma ch'io non t'ami' (but that I should not love you); Rossini's song has 'ah ch'io'. The closing couplet is Rossini's text, not the libretto's, whose second half begins 'Crudele, in che t'offendo'.
