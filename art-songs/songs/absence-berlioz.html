Absence

Absence · Hector Berlioz, Les nuits d'été, Op. 7, No. 4 (1840–41)
Poem: Théophile Gautier (1811–1872)

Reviens, reviens, ma bien-aimée!
Comme une fleur loin du soleil,
La fleur de ma vie est fermée
Loin de ton sourire vermeil.
Come back, come back, my beloved!
Like a flower far from the sun,
the flower of my life is closed,
far from your rosy smile.
Entre nos cœurs quelle distance!
Tant d'espace entre nos baisers!
Ô sort amer! ô dure absence!
Ô grands désirs inapaisés!
Between our hearts, what a distance!
So much space between our kisses!
O bitter fate! O harsh absence!
O great unappeased desires!
D'ici là-bas que de campagnes,
Que de villes et de hameaux,
Que de vallons et de montagnes,
À lasser le pied des chevaux!
From here to there, how many fields,
how many towns and hamlets,
how many valleys and mountains,
enough to weary the horses' hooves!
About the song. A lover calls his beloved back across the distance between them. Berlioz sets only the first three of Gautier's seven stanzas and brings the first stanza back as a refrain after the second and the third (not printed again here). Gautier wrote 'Entre nos cœurs tant de distance!'; Berlioz sings 'quelle distance'. 'Vermeil' (rosy, bright red) describes the smile.
