Sebben, crudele

Although, Cruel One · Antonio Caldara, Canzonetta, usually traced to La costanza in amor vince l'inganno (1710/11)
Poem: Anonymous

Sebben, crudele,
mi fai languir,
sempre fedele
ti voglio amar.
Although, cruel one,
you make me languish,
always faithful
I want to love you.
Con la lunghezza
del mio servir
la tua fierezza
saprò stancar.
With the length
of my service
your pride
I shall wear down.
About the song. A lover promises to wear down the beloved's pride by faithful, patient service. The aria, published in Parisotti's Arie antiche (1885), is usually traced to Caldara's pastoral opera La costanza in amor vince l'inganno (Macerata 1710, Rome 1711), but this source has been questioned. 'Servir' is the lover's devoted service of courtly love; 'fierezza' here is haughtiness or pride rather than ferocity.
