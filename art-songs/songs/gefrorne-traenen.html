Gefror'ne Tränen

Frozen Tears · Franz Schubert, Winterreise, D. 911, No. 3 (1827)
Poem: Wilhelm Müller (1794–1827)

Gefror'ne Tropfen fallen
Von meinen Wangen ab:
Ob es mir denn entgangen,
Daß ich geweinet hab'?
Frozen drops are falling
from my cheeks:
can it be that I did not notice
that I have been weeping?
Ei Tränen, meine Tränen,
Und seid ihr gar so lau,
Daß ihr erstarrt zu Eise
Wie kühler Morgentau?
Ah, tears, my tears,
are you really so lukewarm
that you stiffen into ice
like cool morning dew?
Und dringt doch aus der Quelle
Der Brust so glühend heiß,
Als wolltet ihr zerschmelzen
Des ganzen Winters Eis!
And yet you well up from the spring
of my breast so burning hot,
as if you wanted to melt
the whole winter's ice!
About the song. The traveller finds frozen drops on his cheeks and only then realises he has been crying; he wonders how tears that come from so hot a source can freeze like dew. Müller's poem begins the third line 'Und ist's mir denn entgangen'; Schubert set 'Ob es mir denn entgangen' (can it be that I failed to notice). 'Lau' means lukewarm.
