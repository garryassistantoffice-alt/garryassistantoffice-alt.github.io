Automne

Autumn · Gabriel Fauré, Op. 18 No. 3
Poem: Armand Silvestre (1837–1901)

Automne au ciel brumeux, aux horizons navrants,
Aux rapides couchants, aux aurores pâlies,
Je regarde couler, comme l'eau du torrent,
Tes jours faits de mélancolie.
Autumn of misty skies and heart-rending horizons,
of swift sunsets and pale dawns,
I watch, flowing by like the water of a torrent,
your days made of melancholy.
Sur l'aile des regrets mes esprits emportés,
Comme s'il se pouvait que notre âge renaisse!
Parcourent en rêvant les coteaux enchantés,
Où jadis sourit ma jeunesse!
My thoughts, carried away on the wing of regrets,
as if our time of life could be born again!
roam in a dream over the enchanted hillsides
where my youth once smiled!
Je sens, au clair soleil du souvenir vainqueur,
Refleurir en bouquet les roses déliées,
Et monter à mes yeux des larmes, qu'en mon cœur
Mes vingt ans avaient oubliées!
I feel, in the bright sun of triumphant memory,
the scattered roses bloom again into a bouquet,
and tears rising to my eyes, tears that in my heart
my twenty years had forgotten!
About the song. An autumn song of regret, driven by restless repeated chords in the piano. The poet's thoughts fly back to the 'enchanted hillsides' of his youth, and the memory brings back tears he had forgotten at twenty. 'Navrants' means heart-rending; 'mes esprits' is 'my thoughts' or 'my spirits'; 'les roses déliées' are literally 'untied roses', loosened from a bouquet, which here 'bloom again into a bouquet'.
