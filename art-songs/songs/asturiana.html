Asturiana

Asturian Song · Manuel de Falla, Siete canciones populares españolas, No. 3 (1914)
Poem: Spanish folk text

Por ver si me consolaba,
arrimeme a un pino verde,
por ver si me consolaba.
To see if it would console me,
I leaned against a green pine,
to see if it would console me.
Por verme llorar, lloraba;
y el pino, como era verde,
por verme llorar, lloraba.
Seeing me weep, it wept;
and the pine, because it was green,
seeing me weep, it wept.
About the song. A folk song from Asturias in northern Spain. The singer leans on a green pine for comfort, and the pine weeps with him 'because it was green', that is, alive and full of sap. The repeated lines are part of the folk verse form and are printed so in the score and in a 1926 programme. 'Arrimeme' means I leaned (myself) against.
