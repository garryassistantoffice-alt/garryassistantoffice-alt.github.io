Selve amiche

Friendly Woods · Antonio Caldara, Arietta, usually traced to La costanza in amor vince l'inganno (1710/11)
Poem: Anonymous

Selve amiche, ombrose piante,
fido albergo del mio core,
chiede a voi quest'alma amante
qualche pace al suo dolore.
Friendly woods, shady trees,
faithful shelter of my heart,
this loving soul asks of you
some peace for its sorrow.
About the song. Four lines in which a lover asks the woods, the only refuge of the heart, for some peace. Like 'Sebben, crudele', it is usually traced to Caldara's pastoral opera La costanza in amor vince l'inganno, though the source is not certain. 'Piante' is literally 'plants', here trees.
