Dedication · Richard Strauss, Acht Gedichte aus Letzte Blätter, Op. 10, No. 1
Poem: Hermann von Gilm (1812–1864)

Ja, du weißt es, teure Seele,
Daß ich fern von dir mich quäle,
Liebe macht die Herzen krank,
Habe Dank.
Yes, you know it, dear soul,
that I torment myself far from you;
love makes hearts sick,
my thanks to you.
Einst hielt ich, der Freiheit Zecher,
Hoch den Amethysten-Becher,
Und du segnetest den Trank,
Habe Dank.
Once I, a reveller in freedom,
held high the amethyst cup,
and you blessed the drink,
my thanks to you.
Und beschworst darin die Bösen,
Bis ich, was ich nie gewesen,
Heilig, heilig ans Herz dir sank,
Habe Dank.
And you drove out the evil spirits in it,
until I, as I had never been before,
holy, holy, sank upon your heart,
my thanks to you.
About the song. The first of Strauss's eight songs, Op. 10, on poems from Hermann von Gilm's Letzte Blätter (Last Leaves). Each verse ends 'Habe Dank' ('my thanks to you'). Strauss changed Gilm's 'Hielt ich nicht' to 'Einst hielt ich', and Gilm has a single 'Heilig' and 'an das Herz' in the last stanza.
