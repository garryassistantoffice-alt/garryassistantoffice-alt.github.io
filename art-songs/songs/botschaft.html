Message · Johannes Brahms, Op. 47, No. 1 (1868)
Poem: Georg Friedrich Daumer (1800–1875), after Hafis

Wehe, Lüftchen, lind und lieblich
um die Wange der Geliebten,
spiele zart in ihrer Locke,
eile nicht, hinwegzufliehn!
Blow, little breeze, gently and sweetly
around the cheek of my beloved,
play softly in her curls,
do not hurry to fly away!
Tut sie dann vielleicht die Frage,
wie es um mich Armen stehe,
sprich: „Unendlich war sein Wehe,
höchst bedenklich seine Lage;
If she should then perhaps ask
how things stand with poor me,
say: 'Endless was his woe,
his condition most serious;
aber jetzo kann er hoffen,
wieder herrlich aufzuleben,
denn du, Holde, denkst an ihn.“
but now he can hope
to come gloriously back to life,
for you, sweet one, are thinking of him.'
About the song. Daumer's free German version of a poem by the Persian poet Hafis: the lover sends a breeze to play in his beloved's hair and, if she asks, to report that he was gravely ill with love but will now revive, because she thinks of him. 'Jetzo' is an old form of 'jetzt' (now); 'höchst bedenklich seine Lage' is a doctor's phrase, 'his condition most serious'.
