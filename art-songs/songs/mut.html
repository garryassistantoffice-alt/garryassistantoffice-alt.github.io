Mut!

Courage! · Franz Schubert, Winterreise, D. 911, No. 22 (1827)
Poem: Wilhelm Müller (1794–1827)

Fliegt der Schnee mir ins Gesicht,
Schüttl' ich ihn herunter.
Wenn mein Herz im Busen spricht,
Sing' ich hell und munter.
If the snow flies into my face,
I shake it off.
When my heart speaks in my breast,
I sing out bright and cheerful.
Höre nicht, was es mir sagt,
Habe keine Ohren;
Fühle nicht, was es mir klagt,
Klagen ist für Toren.
I don't hear what it tells me,
I have no ears;
I don't feel what it laments to me:
lamenting is for fools.
Lustig in die Welt hinein
Gegen Wind und Wetter!
Will kein Gott auf Erden sein,
Sind wir selber Götter!
Merrily out into the world,
against wind and weather!
If there will be no god on earth,
then we ourselves are gods!
About the song. A burst of defiant cheerfulness: the traveller shakes off the snow, refuses to listen to his heart's complaints, and declares that if there is no god on earth, people must be gods themselves. The 'es' in the second stanza is the heart. Old prints spell the title 'Muth!'.
