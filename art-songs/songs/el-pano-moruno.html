El paño moruno

The Moorish Cloth · Manuel de Falla, Siete canciones populares españolas, No. 1 (1914)
Poem: Spanish folk text

Al paño fino, en la tienda,
una mancha le cayó;
por menos precio se vende,
porque perdió su valor.
¡Ay!
On the fine cloth in the shop
a stain has fallen;
it sells for a lower price
because it has lost its value.
Ah!
About the song. The first of Falla's seven arrangements of Spanish folk songs, a set dedicated to Ida Godebska. A fine cloth in a shop gets a stain and must be sold cheaply: an image for a reputation that has been spoiled. 'Moruno' means Moorish.
