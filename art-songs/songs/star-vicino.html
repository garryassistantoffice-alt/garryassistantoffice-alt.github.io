Star vicino

To Be Near · Attributed to Salvator Rosa, Arietta
Poem: Anonymous

Star vicino al bell'idol che s'ama
è il più vago diletto d'amor.
To be near the lovely idol one loves
is love's most charming delight.
Star lontano dal ben che si brama
è d'amore il più vivo dolor.
To be far from the beloved one longs for
is love's most piercing pain.
About the song. A two-line maxim about love, answered by a second stanza: being near the beloved is love's sweetest delight, being far away its sharpest pain. The attribution is weak: Floridia's Early Italian Songs and Airs (1923) prints the song as by Salvator Rosa (1615–1673) and says in a footnote that the second stanza was added later by 'Count Pepoli of Bologna', but we have found neither claim documented in an earlier source, so we list the music as attributed to Rosa and the words as anonymous. 'Vago' means lovely, charming, not vague.
