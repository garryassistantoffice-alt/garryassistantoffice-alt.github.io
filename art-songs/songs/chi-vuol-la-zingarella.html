Chi vuol la zingarella

Who Wants the Gypsy Girl · Giovanni Paisiello, Canzone, from the opera I zingari in fiera (Naples, 1789)
Poem: Giuseppe Palomba (librettist of the opera)

Chi vuol la zingarella
graziosa, accorta e bella?
Signori, eccola qua.
Who wants the gypsy girl,
charming, clever and pretty?
Gentlemen, here she is.
Le donne sul balcone
so bene indovinar;
i giovani al cantone
so meglio stuzzicar.
A vecchi innamorati
scaldar fo le cervella.
The ladies on the balcony
I know well how to read;
the young men on the corner
I know even better how to tease.
Old men in love
I set their brains on fire.
About the song. A gypsy girl advertises herself at the fair: she reads the fortunes of the ladies on their balconies, teases the young men on the street corner and turns the heads of old men in love. 'Al cantone' means at the street corner; 'scaldar le cervella' is literally 'to heat up their brains'. Parisotti names the opera 'Gli Zingari in fiera'.
