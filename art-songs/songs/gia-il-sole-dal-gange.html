Già il sole dal Gange

Already the Sun from the Ganges · Alessandro Scarlatti, L'honestà negli amori (Rome, 1680)
Poem: Librettist uncertain (published under the pseudonym Felice Parnasso; a member of the Bernini family has been suggested)

Già il sole dal Gange
più chiaro sfavilla,
e terge ogni stilla
dell'alba che piange.
Already the sun from the Ganges
sparkles more brightly,
and dries every drop
of the dawn, which weeps.
Col raggio dorato
ingemma ogni stelo,
e gli astri del cielo
dipinge nel prato.
With its golden ray
it bejewels every stem,
and the stars of the sky
it paints on the meadow.
About the song. A bright canzonetta from one of Scarlatti's first operas, written when he was nineteen. The sun rises in the east, over the Ganges, and dries the dew, pictured as the tears of the weeping dawn. 'Ingemma' means 'sets with jewels', 'bejewels'.
