Nel cor più non mi sento

In My Heart I No Longer Feel · Giovanni Paisiello, L'amor contrastato (La molinara) (Naples, 1788)
Poem: Giuseppe Palomba

Nel cor più non mi sento
brillar la gioventù;
cagion del mio tormento,
amor, sei colpa tu.
In my heart I no longer feel
youth sparkling;
cause of my torment,
love, it is your fault.
Mi pizzichi, mi stuzzichi,
mi pungichi, mi mastichi;
che cosa è questo, ahimè?
Pietà, pietà, pietà!
Amore è un certo che,
che disperar mi fa!
You pinch me, you tease me,
you prick me, you chew me;
what is this, alas?
Mercy, mercy, mercy!
Love is a certain something
that drives me to despair!
About the song. A tune from Paisiello's comic opera L'amor contrastato, better known as La molinara (The Miller Girl); it was so popular that Beethoven wrote piano variations on it (WoO 70). The second verse is comic patter. 'Pungichi' comes from 'pungicare', an old or regional form of 'pungere' (to prick, to sting), matching the other playful verbs; 'mi mastichi' is literally 'you chew me'.
