La gita in gondola

The Gondola Trip · Gioachino Rossini, Soirées musicales, No. 7 (publ. 1835)
Poem: Carlo Pepoli (1796–1881)

Voli l'agile barchetta,
voga, voga, o marinar:
or ch'Elvira, mia diletta,
a me in braccio sfida il mar.
Brilla in calma la laguna,
una vela non appar;
pallidetta è in ciel la luna,
tutto invita a sospirar.
Voga, voga, marinar.
Let the nimble little boat fly,
row, row, O boatman,
now that Elvira, my beloved,
in my arms defies the sea.
The lagoon shines in the calm,
not a sail appears;
palish in the sky is the moon,
everything invites us to sigh.
Row, row, boatman.
Se ad un bacio amor t'invita,
non temer, mio bel tesor;
tu saprai che sia la vita
sol nel bacio dell'amor.
Ma già un zeffiro sereno
dolce dolce ondeggia il mar:
vieni, Elvira, a questo seno,
vieni e apprendi a palpitar.
Voga, voga, marinar.
If love invites you to a kiss,
do not be afraid, my lovely treasure;
you will learn what life is
only in the kiss of love.
But already a calm breeze
sweetly, sweetly rocks the sea:
come, Elvira, to this breast,
come and learn to throb with love.
Row, row, boatman.
About the song. A barcarolle on the Venetian lagoon: the lover urges the gondolier on while Elvira lies in his arms. 'Voga' is the imperative of 'vogare', to row; 'zeffiro' is a soft west wind, any gentle breeze. 'Apprendi a palpitar' is 'learn to throb', to feel the heart beat with love. Rossini repeats several lines; we print each once.
