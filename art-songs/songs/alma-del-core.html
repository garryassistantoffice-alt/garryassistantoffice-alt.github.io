Alma del core

Soul of My Heart · Antonio Caldara, Aria, usually traced to La costanza in amor vince l'inganno (1710/11)
Poem: Anonymous

Alma del core,
spirto dell'alma,
sempre costante
t'adorerò!
Soul of my heart,
spirit of my soul,
always constant
I will adore you!
Sarò contento
nel mio tormento
se quel bel labbro
baciar potrò.
I shall be content
in my torment
if those lovely lips
I may kiss.
About the song. A minuet-like love song in which the lover will be content in his torment if only he may kiss the beloved's lips. The source is usually given as Caldara's pastoral opera La costanza in amor vince l'inganno, but this is not certain. 'Alma' is the usual poetic word for soul.
