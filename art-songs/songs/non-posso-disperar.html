Non posso disperar

I Cannot Despair · Attributed to S. De Luca, Arietta
Poem: Anonymous

Non posso disperar,
sei troppo cara al cor.
I cannot despair,
you are too dear to my heart.
Il solo sperare
d'aver a gioire
m'è un dolce languire,
m'è un caro dolor.
Merely hoping
that I shall rejoice
is a sweet languishing to me,
is a dear pain to me.
About the song. The lover cannot give up hope, because the beloved is too dear to his heart; merely hoping for joy is a sweet languishing, a dear pain. Parisotti (1894) gives the composer only as 'S. De Luca' and says the arietta was found among 17th-century manuscripts; nothing else is known of him there.
