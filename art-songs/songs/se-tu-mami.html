Se tu m'ami

If You Love Me · Attributed to Giovanni Battista Pergolesi; probably by Alessandro Parisotti, Arietta (published 1885)
Poem: Paolo Rolli (1687–1765)

Se tu m'ami, se sospiri
sol per me, gentil pastor,
ho dolor de' tuoi martiri,
ho diletto del tuo amor;
ma se pensi che soletto
io ti debba riamar,
pastorello, sei soggetto
facilmente a t'ingannar.
If you love me, if you sigh
only for me, gentle shepherd,
I feel sorrow for your sufferings,
I take delight in your love;
but if you think that you alone
I must love in return,
little shepherd, you are liable
easily to deceive yourself.
Bella rosa porporina
oggi Silvia sceglierà;
con la scusa della spina
doman poi la sprezzerà.
A beautiful crimson rose
Silvia will choose today;
with the thorn as her excuse
tomorrow she will scorn it.
Ma degli uomini il consiglio
io per me non seguirò.
Non perché mi piace il giglio
gli altri fiori sprezzerò.
But the advice of men
I, for my part, will not follow.
Just because I like the lily
I will not scorn the other flowers.
About the song. Long printed under Pergolesi's name, this song was most likely composed by its editor, Alessandro Parisotti, who published it in his Arie antiche (1885); no earlier source is known. The words are a canzonetta by Paolo Rolli. A shepherdess teases her suitor: she likes his love, but has no intention of loving only him.
