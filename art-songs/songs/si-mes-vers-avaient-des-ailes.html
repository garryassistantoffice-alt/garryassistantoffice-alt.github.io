Si mes vers avaient des ailes

If My Verses Had Wings · Reynaldo Hahn, Mélodie
Poem: Victor Hugo (1802–1885), from Les Contemplations

Mes vers fuiraient, doux et frêles,
Vers votre jardin si beau,
Si mes vers avaient des ailes,
Comme l'oiseau!
My verses would fly, sweet and frail,
to your garden so fair,
if my verses had wings,
like the bird!
Ils voleraient, étincelles,
Vers votre foyer qui rit,
Si mes vers avaient des ailes,
Comme l'esprit!
They would fly like sparks
to your laughing hearth,
if my verses had wings,
like the mind!
Près de vous, purs et fidèles,
Ils accourraient nuit et jour,
Si mes vers avaient des ailes,
Comme l'amour!
Near you, pure and faithful,
they would hasten night and day,
if my verses had wings,
like love!
About the song. Three short stanzas in which the poet's verses would fly to the beloved like a bird, like the mind, like love. Hugo's refrain line is 'Des ailes comme l'oiseau' (wings like the bird); Hahn sets only 'Comme l'oiseau', and likewise in the other stanzas. 'L'esprit' can mean the mind, wit or spirit.
