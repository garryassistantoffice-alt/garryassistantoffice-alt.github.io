В тёмной роще замолк соловей

In the Dark Grove the Nightingale Has Fallen Silent · Nikolai Rimsky-Korsakov, Four Romances, Op. 4 No. 3 (1866)
Poem: Ivan Nikitin (1824–1861)

В тёмной роще замолк соловей,
Прокатилась по небу звезда;
Месяц смотрит сквозь чащу ветвей,
Зажигает росу на траве.
In the dark grove the nightingale has fallen silent,
a star has rolled across the sky;
the moon looks through the thicket of branches
and kindles the dew on the grass.
Как при месяце кроток и тих
У тебя милый очерк лица!
Эту ночь, полный грёз золотых,
Я б продлил без конца, без конца!
How gentle and still in the moonlight
is the dear outline of your face!
This night, full of golden dreams,
I would make last without end, without end!
About the song. A still moonlit night: the nightingale has stopped, a star falls, the moon lights the dew, and the lover wishes the night would never end. Nikitin's collected poems print a longer version ('В тёмной чаще', 'звезда в синеве', 'сквозь сетку ветвей', with a middle stanza); we print the words in Rimsky-Korsakov's score. 'Очерк лица' is the outline of a face. The song is dedicated to César Cui.
