Caro laccio, dolce nodo

Dear Snare, Sweet Knot · Francesco Gasparini, Arietta
Poem: Anonymous

Caro laccio, dolce nodo,
che legasti il mio pensier,
so ch'io peno e pur ne godo,
son contento e prigionier.
Dear snare, sweet knot,
that have bound my thoughts,
I know that I suffer, and yet I enjoy it;
I am content and a prisoner.
About the song. The lover blesses the 'snare' and 'knot' of love that have bound his thoughts: he suffers and yet enjoys it, a contented prisoner. Parisotti prints it as a 'fragment from the second cantata', without naming the collection.
