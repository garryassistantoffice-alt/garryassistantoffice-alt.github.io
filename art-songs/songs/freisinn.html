Freisinn

Free Spirit · Robert Schumann, Myrthen, Op. 25, No. 2 (1840)
Poem: Johann Wolfgang von Goethe (1749–1832), from West-östlicher Divan

Laßt mich nur auf meinem Sattel gelten!
Bleibt in euren Hütten, euren Zelten!
Und ich reite froh in alle Ferne,
über meiner Mütze nur die Sterne.
Just let me hold my own in my saddle!
Stay in your huts, your tents!
And I ride gladly into every distance,
with only the stars above my cap.
Er hat euch die Gestirne gesetzt
als Leiter zu Land und See,
damit ihr euch daran ergötzt,
stets blickend in die Höh'.
He has set the stars for you
as guides on land and sea,
so that you may delight in them,
always looking upward.
About the song. A rider's declaration of independence from the 'Book of the Singer' in Goethe's West-östlicher Divan (1819): let others stay in their huts and tents, he wants nothing over his head but the stars. 'Gelten' is to count for something, to be valued; 'ergötzen' is an old word for to delight. Schumann repeats the first verse at the end.
