Sérénade toscane

Tuscan Serenade · Gabriel Fauré, Op. 3, No. 2
Poem: Romain Bussine (1830–1899), after an anonymous Italian poem

Ô toi que berce un rêve enchanteur,
Tu dors tranquille en ton lit solitaire,
Éveille-toi, regarde le chanteur,
Esclave de tes yeux, dans la nuit claire!
O you whom an enchanting dream is rocking,
you sleep peacefully in your solitary bed;
wake up, look at the singer,
slave of your eyes, in the clear night!
Éveille-toi, mon âme, ma pensée,
Entends ma voix par la brise emportée:
Entends ma voix chanter!
Entends ma voix pleurer, dans la rosée!
Wake up, my soul, my thought,
hear my voice carried off by the breeze:
hear my voice singing!
Hear my voice weeping, in the dew!
Sous ta fenêtre en vain ma voix expire,
Et chaque nuit je redis mon martyre,
Sans autre abri que la voûte étoilée.
Le vent brise ma voix et la nuit est glacée.
Beneath your window my voice dies away in vain,
and every night I tell my torment again,
with no other shelter than the starry vault.
The wind breaks my voice, and the night is icy.
Mon chant s'éteint en un accent suprême,
Ma lèvre tremble en murmurant: je t'aime!
Je ne peux plus chanter!
Ah! daigne te montrer! daigne apparaître!
My song dies out in a last cry,
my lips tremble as they murmur: I love you!
I can sing no more!
Ah, deign to show yourself! Deign to appear!
Si j'étais sûr que tu ne veux paraître,
Je m'en irais, pour t'oublier, demander au sommeil
De me bercer jusqu'au matin vermeil,
De me bercer jusqu'à ne plus t'aimer!
If I were sure that you will not appear,
I would go away and, to forget you, ask sleep
to rock me until the rosy morning,
to rock me until I love you no more!
About the song. A singer under a window begs his sleeping beloved to wake and show herself; if she will not, he will ask sleep to cure him of loving her. The French words are Bussine's version of an anonymous Tuscan poem, and the old Hamelle edition prints the Italian words under the French, headed 'Poésie toscane'. 'Martyre' is the lover's torment; 'le matin vermeil' is the rosy dawn.
