Morgengruß

Morning Greeting · Franz Schubert, Die schöne Müllerin, D. 795, No. 8 (1823)
Poem: Wilhelm Müller (1794–1827)

Guten Morgen, schöne Müllerin!
Wo steckst du gleich das Köpfchen hin,
Als wär' dir was geschehen?
Verdrießt dich denn mein Gruß so schwer?
Verstört dich denn mein Blick so sehr?
So muß ich wieder gehen.
Good morning, lovely miller's daughter!
Why do you hide your little head at once,
as if something had happened to you?
Does my greeting annoy you so badly?
Does my glance upset you so much?
Then I must go away again.
O laß mich nur von ferne steh'n,
Nach deinem lieben Fenster seh'n,
Von ferne, ganz von ferne!
Du blondes Köpfchen, komm hervor!
Hervor aus eurem runden Tor,
Ihr blauen Morgensterne!
Oh, only let me stand at a distance
and look up at your dear window,
from afar, from very far!
Little fair head, come out!
Come out of your round gates,
you blue morning stars!
Ihr schlummertrunk'nen Äugelein,
Ihr taubetrübten Blümelein,
Was scheuet ihr die Sonne?
Hat es die Nacht so gut gemeint,
Daß ihr euch schließt und bückt und weint
Nach ihrer stillen Wonne?
You little eyes, drunk with sleep,
you little flowers, dimmed with dew,
why do you shy away from the sun?
Was the night so kind to you
that you close and droop and weep
for its silent bliss?
Nun schüttelt ab der Träume Flor
Und hebt euch frisch und frei empor
In Gottes hellen Morgen!
Die Lerche wirbelt in der Luft,
Und aus dem tiefen Herzen ruft
Die Liebe Leid und Sorgen.
Now shake off the veil of dreams
and rise up fresh and free
into God's bright morning!
The lark is warbling in the air,
and from the depths of the heart
love calls out its sorrow and cares.
About the song. He greets the miller's daughter at her window; she draws her head back, and he begs only to look from a distance, calling her blue eyes out like flowers opening to the sun. 'Morgensterne' and 'Äugelein' are her eyes, and their 'round gate' her eyelids; 'Flor' is a thin veil of gauze.
