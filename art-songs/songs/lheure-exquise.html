L'heure exquise

The Exquisite Hour · Reynaldo Hahn, Chansons grises, No. 5
Poem: Paul Verlaine (1844–1896), from La bonne chanson

La lune blanche
Luit dans les bois;
De chaque branche
Part une voix
Sous la ramée...
Ô bien-aimée.
The white moon
shines in the woods;
from every branch
a voice rises
beneath the boughs...
O beloved.
L'étang reflète,
Profond miroir,
La silhouette
Du saule noir
Où le vent pleure...
Rêvons, c'est l'heure.
The pond reflects,
a deep mirror,
the silhouette
of the black willow
where the wind is weeping...
Let us dream, it is the hour.
Un vaste et tendre
Apaisement
Semble descendre
Du firmament
Que l'astre irise...
C'est l'heure exquise.
A vast and tender
peace
seems to descend
from the firmament
that the star makes iridescent...
It is the exquisite hour.
About the song. Verlaine's moonlit poem 'La lune blanche' from La bonne chanson, set by Hahn as the fifth of his Chansons grises over a gently rippling piano. 'Part une voix' means 'a voice rises' (partir, to set out), not 'parts'; 'la ramée' is the leafy boughs; 'l'astre' (literally 'the star' or heavenly body) is the moon.
