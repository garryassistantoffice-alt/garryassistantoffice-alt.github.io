Tu lo sai

You Know It · Attributed to Giuseppe Torelli, Arietta (date uncertain)
Poem: Anonymous

Tu lo sai quanto t'amai,
tu lo sai, lo sai, crudel!
Io non bramo altra mercè,
ma ricordati di me,
e poi sprezza un infedel.
You know how much I loved you,
you know it, you know it, cruel one!
I long for no other reward,
only remember me,
and then scorn an unfaithful one.
About the song. Traditionally credited to the Bolognese violinist-composer Giuseppe Torelli (1658–1709), though the attribution is doubtful. The lover asks nothing except to be remembered. The last line is ambiguous: literally 'and then scorn an unfaithful one'; it is often understood bitterly, 'and then go on scorning me, as you would someone faithless'.
