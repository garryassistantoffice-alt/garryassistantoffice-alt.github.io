Canción

Song · Manuel de Falla, Siete canciones populares españolas, No. 6 (1914)
Poem: Spanish folk text

Por traidores, tus ojos,
voy a enterrarlos;
no sabes lo que cuesta,
«del aire»,
niña, el mirarlos.
«Madre, a la orilla.»
Because they are traitors, your eyes,
I am going to bury them;
you do not know what it costs,
'del aire',
girl, to look at them.
'Mother, to the shore.'
Dicen que no me quieres,
ya me has querido...
Váyase lo ganado,
«del aire»,
por lo perdido.
«Madre, a la orilla.»
They say you do not love me,
yet once you did love me...
Let what was won go
'del aire'
against what was lost.
'Mother, to the shore.'
About the song. A teasing love song with sung interjections: 'del aire' (literally 'of the air', a stock refrain filler) and 'Madre, a la orilla' (Mother, to the shore). 'Váyase lo ganado por lo perdido' is a saying: let what was gained make up for what was lost. Falla repeats several lines; we print each line once.
