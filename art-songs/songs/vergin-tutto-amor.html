Vergin, tutto amor

Virgin, All Love · Attributed to Francesco Durante, Preghiera (prayer); vocal line from a Durante solfeggio, words added later
Poem: Anonymous (19th century?)

Vergin, tutto amor,
o madre di bontade,
o madre pia,
ascolta, dolce Maria,
la voce del peccator.
Virgin, all love,
O mother of goodness,
O merciful mother,
hear, sweet Mary,
the voice of the sinner.
Il pianto suo ti muova,
giungano a te i suoi lamenti,
suo duol, suoi tristi accenti;
senti pietoso quel tuo cor.
Let his weeping move you,
let his laments reach you,
his grief, his sad words;
let that heart of yours feel compassion.
About the song. Like 'Danza, danza', this prayer to the Virgin Mary seems to be one of Durante's wordless solfeggi with a text added later, probably in the 19th century. 'Pia' means loving, merciful, not only 'pious'. The last line is compressed; literally 'feel, compassionate, that heart of yours'.
