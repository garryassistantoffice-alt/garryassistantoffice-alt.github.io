O cessate di piagarmi

Oh, Stop Wounding Me · Alessandro Scarlatti, Il Pompeo (Rome, 1683)
Poem: Nicolò Minato (c. 1627–1698), adapted from his Pompeo Magno

O cessate di piagarmi,
o lasciatemi morir!
Luci ingrate, dispietate,
più del gelo e più de' marmi
fredde e sorde a' miei martir.
Oh, stop wounding me,
or let me die!
Ungrateful, pitiless eyes,
more than ice and more than marble
cold and deaf to my torments.
About the song. An arietta from Scarlatti's early opera Il Pompeo: the singer begs the beloved's cold eyes either to stop wounding or to let him die. The Schirmer anthology prints 'più del marmi', an apparent misprint; we print 'de' marmi' (= dei marmi, than marble). 'Luci' (lights) is the usual poetic word for eyes.
