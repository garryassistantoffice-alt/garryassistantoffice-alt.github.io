Le violette

The Violets · Alessandro Scarlatti, Pirro e Demetrio (Naples, 1694)
Poem: Adriano Morselli (d. c. 1691)

Rugiadose, odorose,
violette graziose,
voi vi state vergognose,
mezzo ascose fra le foglie,
e sgridate le mie voglie,
che son troppo ambiziose.
Dewy, fragrant,
graceful violets,
you stay there bashfully,
half hidden among the leaves,
and you rebuke my desires,
which are too ambitious.
About the song. An aria from Scarlatti's opera Pirro e Demetrio. The modest violets, half hidden in their leaves, seem to rebuke the singer for wanting too much. The aria is known as 'Le violette', but its text begins 'Rugiadose, odorose'.
