Im Rhein, im heiligen Strome

In the Rhine, the Holy River · Robert Schumann, Dichterliebe, Op. 48, No. 6 (1840)
Poem: Heinrich Heine (1797–1856), from Lyrisches Intermezzo

Im Rhein, im heiligen Strome,
da spiegelt sich in den Well'n,
mit seinem großen Dome,
das große, heilige Köln.
In the Rhine, in the holy river,
there is mirrored in the waves,
with its great cathedral,
great, holy Cologne.
Im Dom da steht ein Bildnis,
auf goldenem Leder gemalt;
in meines Lebens Wildnis
hat's freundlich hineingestrahlt.
In the cathedral there hangs a picture
painted on gilded leather;
into the wilderness of my life
it has shone kindly.
Es schweben Blumen und Englein
um unsre liebe Frau;
die Augen, die Lippen, die Wänglein,
die gleichen der Liebsten genau.
Flowers and little angels hover
around Our Lady;
the eyes, the lips, the little cheeks
are exactly like my beloved's.
About the song. Cologne Cathedral is reflected in the Rhine; inside hangs a painting of the Virgin Mary on gilded leather, and her face is exactly the beloved's. 'Unsre liebe Frau' (Our Lady) is the Virgin Mary; 'Köln' was then spelled 'Cöln'. The words are as in Heine's Buch der Lieder.
