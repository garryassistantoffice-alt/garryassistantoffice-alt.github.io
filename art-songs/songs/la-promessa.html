La promessa

The Promise · Gioachino Rossini, Soirées musicales, No. 1 (publ. 1835)
Poem: Pietro Metastasio (1698–1782)

Ch'io mai vi possa
lasciar d'amare,
no, nol credete,
pupille care;
nemmen per gioco
v'ingannerò.
That I could ever
stop loving you,
no, do not believe it,
dear eyes;
not even in jest
will I deceive you.
Voi sole siete
le mie faville,
e voi sarete,
care pupille,
il mio bel foco
sin ch'io vivrò.
You alone are
my sparks,
and you will be,
dear eyes,
my lovely fire
as long as I live.
About the song. The first song of Rossini's Soirées musicales, a set of eight solo songs and four duets. A lover swears to his beloved's eyes that he will never stop loving them, 'not even in jest'. The lines also appear in Metastasio's Siroe, where the librettos of 1728 (London) and 1740 (Rome) read 'Non lo credete' instead of 'No, nol credete'. 'Faville' are sparks, the eyes that set the lover alight.
