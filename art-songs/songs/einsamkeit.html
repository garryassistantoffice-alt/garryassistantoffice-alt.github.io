Einsamkeit

Loneliness · Franz Schubert, Winterreise, D. 911, No. 12 (1827)
Poem: Wilhelm Müller (1794–1827)

Wie eine trübe Wolke
Durch heitre Lüfte geht,
Wenn in der Tanne Wipfel
Ein mattes Lüftchen weht:
As a dark cloud
passes through clear skies
when in the top of the fir tree
a faint breeze is blowing:
So zieh' ich meine Straße
Dahin mit trägem Fuß,
Durch helles, frohes Leben,
Einsam und ohne Gruß.
so I go on my way
with sluggish step,
through bright, happy life,
alone and without a greeting.
Ach, daß die Luft so ruhig!
Ach, daß die Welt so licht!
Als noch die Stürme tobten,
War ich so elend nicht.
Ah, that the air should be so calm!
Ah, that the world should be so bright!
While the storms were still raging,
I was not so wretched.
About the song. Like a dark cloud drifting across a clear sky, the traveller passes through the bright, cheerful world with no one to greet him; he was less wretched while the storms were raging. 'Trüb' means dull, gloomy; 'matt' means faint. Müller wrote 'Wann in der Tanne Wipfel'; Schubert sets 'Wenn'.
