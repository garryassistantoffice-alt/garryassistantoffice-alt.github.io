Wandrers Nachtlied (Über allen Gipfeln)

Wanderer's Night Song (Over All the Hilltops) · Franz Schubert, D. 768, Op. 96, No. 3 (published 1828)
Poem: Johann Wolfgang von Goethe (1749–1832)

Über allen Gipfeln
Ist Ruh,
In allen Wipfeln
Spürest du
Kaum einen Hauch;
Die Vöglein schweigen im Walde.
Warte nur, balde
Ruhest du auch.
Over all the hilltops
is rest,
in all the treetops
you feel
hardly a breath;
the little birds are silent in the forest.
Only wait, soon
you too will rest.
About the song. Goethe is said to have written these eight lines in pencil on the wall of a hunting hut on the Kickelhahn above Ilmenau in September 1780. Schubert's setting is one of his shortest songs; he repeats the last lines, which we print once. Goethe's own text has 'Die Vögelein'; Schubert sings the shorter 'Die Vöglein'. 'Balde' is an old form of 'bald', soon.
