La partenza

The Parting · Gioachino Rossini, Soirées musicales, No. 3 (publ. 1835)
Poem: Pietro Metastasio (1698–1782)

Ecco quel fiero istante,
Nice, mia Nice, addio;
come vivrò, ben mio,
così lontan da te?
Io vivrò sempre in pene,
io non avrò più bene;
e tu, chi sa se mai
ti sovverrai di me!
Here is that cruel moment:
Nice, my Nice, farewell;
how shall I live, my love,
so far away from you?
I shall live always in pain,
I shall have no more happiness;
and you, who knows if ever
you will remember me!
Sempre nel tuo cammino,
sempre m'avrai vicino;
e tu, chi sa se mai
ti sovverrai di me!
Always on your way
you will have me near you;
and you, who knows if ever
you will remember me!
About the song. Metastasio's canzonetta La partenza is a long farewell to Nice (the lover names himself Fileno near the end), almost every stanza ending 'and you, who knows if you will ever remember me'. Rossini sets only the first stanza and the last four lines of the second. 'Ti sovverrai di me' is an old literary form of 'ti ricorderai di me'.
