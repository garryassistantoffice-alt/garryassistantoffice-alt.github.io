Malìa

Enchantment · Francesco Paolo Tosti, Melodia (1887)
Poem: Rocco Emanuele Pagliara (d. 1914)

Cosa c'era nel fior che m'hai dato?
Forse un filtro, un arcano poter!
Nel toccarlo, 'l mio core ha tremato,
m'ha l'olezzo turbato 'l pensier!
What was in the flower you gave me?
Perhaps a potion, a secret power!
When I touched it, my heart trembled,
its scent troubled my thoughts!
Nelle vaghe movenze che ci hai?
Un incanto vien forse con te?
Freme l'aria per dove tu vai,
spunta un fiore ove passa 'l tuo piè!
What is it you have in your graceful movements?
Does some enchantment come with you?
The air quivers wherever you go,
a flower springs up where your foot passes!
Io non chiedo qual plaga beata
fino adesso soggiorno ti fu:
non ti chiedo se ninfa, se fata,
se una bionda parvenza sei tu!
I do not ask what blessed land
has been your home until now;
I do not ask whether you are a nymph, a fairy,
or a fair-haired apparition!
Ma che c'è nel tuo sguardo fatale?
Cosa ci hai nel tuo magico dir?
Se mi guardi, un'ebbrezza m'assale,
se mi parli, mi sento morir!
But what is there in your fateful gaze?
What is it you have in your magic speech?
If you look at me, a rapture seizes me,
if you speak to me, I feel I am dying!
About the song. 'Malìa' is a spell, a bewitchment. A man asks what power lies in the flower, the walk, the gaze and the voice of the woman he has met. Tosti dedicated the song to Édouard de Reszke. 'Plaga' here is a poetic word for a region, not a wound; 'olezzo' is fragrance. Ricordi's print has 'ne'l', 'ne le' and the elided ''l'; we modernise the first two and keep the elision. Tosti repeats the last two lines of the second and fourth verses.
