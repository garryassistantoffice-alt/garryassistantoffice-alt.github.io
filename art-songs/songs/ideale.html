Ideale

Ideal · Francesco Paolo Tosti, Melodia (Milan: Ricordi)
Poem: Carmelo Errico (1848–1892)

Io ti seguii come iride di pace
lungo le vie del cielo;
io ti seguii come un'amica face
de la notte nel velo.
I followed you like a rainbow of peace
along the paths of the sky;
I followed you like a friendly torch
in the veil of the night.
E ti sentii ne la luce, ne l'aria,
nel profumo dei fiori;
e fu piena la stanza solitaria
di te, dei tuoi splendori.
And I felt you in the light, in the air,
in the scent of the flowers;
and the lonely room was filled
with you, with your radiance.
In te rapito, al suon de la tua voce,
lungamente sognai;
e de la terra ogni affanno, ogni croce,
in quel giorno scordai.
Enraptured by you, at the sound of your voice,
I dreamed for a long time;
and every anguish, every cross of this earth
I forgot on that day.
Torna, caro ideal, torna un istante
a sorridermi ancora,
e a me risplenderà, nel tuo sembiante,
una novella aurora.
Come back, dear ideal, come back for a moment
to smile at me again,
and in your face there will shine for me
a new dawn.
About the song. The poet remembers following an ideal, a vision of love, through sky and night, and begs it to return for one moment. Ricordi's printed score sets 'In quel giorno scordai' (I forgot on that day), while the poem printed on the page before the music reads 'In quel sogno scordai' (I forgot in that dream); we print what Tosti set. 'Face' is an old word for a torch, and 'iride' here is a rainbow.
